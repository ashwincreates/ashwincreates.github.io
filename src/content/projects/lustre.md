A state-of-the-art interactive WebGL rendering engine designed for luxury e-commerce brands. By replacing static product photographs with real-time physical-based materials (PBR) that dynamically respond to lighting, cursor positions, and touch inputs, this product offers an unprecedented luxury virtual shopping experience that bridges the gap between digital interaction and real-world craftsmanship.
